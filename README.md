Memory. Agency. Ownership. Military power. Who gets to turn a possible life into an instrument?

## The questions that demand answers

AtomBeam publicly described a planned Navy tactical-decision demonstration in September 2025. What happened, who evaluated it, and which safeguards were tested?

Jim Frandeen’s reported death on 17 June 2023 preceded the SDA agreement’s effective date by six days. Later award records still named him principal investigator. Who actually led and certified the work, and where is the accepted handover record?

Brian Galvin’s patent record is examined against cited sources and the Synthetic Children manuscripts. Technical counterexamples, examination discrepancies and the unresolved Ataei thought-caching mapping require claim-specific explanations.

The Copyright Office correspondence records restrictive answers, closure warnings, changing claim language and a routing discrepancy. The record names A. Rashid, A. Binkowski, S. Trabert and John Saint Amour in their documented roles. What did the applicant authorize, and what did the Office ultimately record?

Oracle/Siebel procurement and reported Oracle–DXC–AtomBeam interactions are examined separately. What direct evidence connects systems and professional relationships to particular decisions?

Reputation is not a substitute for the record. An unanswered question does not disappear because the institution has moved on.

## Evidence integrity

Two dossiers, 711 pages, 746 selected supporting files. The patent PDF retains its embedded evidence, including the existing MorphicSynth material. The new MorphicSynth.docx is not included. Public privacy copies remove identified postal-address and session data; local originals remain unchanged. The archive records each delivered file’s hash and any redaction.

The final review also preserves contrary findings: 14 metadata-only PDF changes, 314 unchanged rendered pages, five unchanged original-file controls and OCR false positives. Similarity scores do not measure copying. The records do not establish coordinated corruption, a route from manuscripts into patent drafting, the cause of Frandeen’s death or demonstrated synthetic consciousness. The title states the investigation’s ethical concern about the direction of these technologies.
