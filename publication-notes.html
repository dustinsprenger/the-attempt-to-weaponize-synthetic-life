How to read the evidence

The final accuracy review governs earlier analytical statements in the evidence package. Patent-family dates, publication dates, local file dates and capture dates describe different events. Feature-overlap scores are ordinal analytical judgments, not percentages of copying. Named relationships do not themselves establish coordinated conduct.

The privacy review used local extraction, existing OCR, container inspection and visual review. Some scanned public source pages lack reliable text recognition; the review is bounded and is not a guarantee that every printed character has been recognised.

Corrections and preservation
